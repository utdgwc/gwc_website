Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time commitment for GWC projects?

The time commitment for GWC projects varies depending on the project requirements and deadline. Here at GWC, we highly value mental health, so we expect around 3-4 hours per week to leave you enough time for other commitments!

Do I need to have prior coding experience to join a GWC project?

No prior coding experience is required to join our Projects Team! We encourage members of all skill levels and backgrounds to join us and learn new skills together.

How do I apply for a GWC project?

You can apply for a GWC project by clicking the "Join Project" button on this Project page and filling out the application form with your interests, availability, and any relevant experience.

What if I need help with my project tasks?

If you need help with your project tasks, feel free to reach out to your PM or fellow team members for support. We are always here to help you learn and grow!
