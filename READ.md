# Girls Who Code 

Girls Who Code (GWC) is a national organization that is dedicated to lowering the gender gap in the tech industry by providing free summer programs for high school students in AI, data science, game design, and more. See https://girlswhocode.com/ for more information!

## UTD Girls Who Code

UTD GWC is on a mission to create a community in the tech space for women who are college students! 🧡💚


### About

This website serves as a professional display of what GWC is about and what we have to offer our community. It showcases our events, officers, and mission to companies and prospective members.
